End-to-End Setup Guide

1

Download Zebra Browser Print

This application acts as a bridge between this website and your printer hardware. Select your operating system below:

Windows macOS
2

Configure USB Printer

Open Browser Print, right-click the tray icon (Windows) or menu bar icon (mac), and select Settings. Ensure your printer is selected in the "Default Device" dropdown.

3

Grant Permission

When you click "Print" for the first time, Browser Print will ask for permission to allow this site to access your printer. Click Allow.
